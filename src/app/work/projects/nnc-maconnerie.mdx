## Overview

Developed a complete online presence for N.N.C Maçonnerie, a professional masonry artisan based in Saint-Brieuc (Brittany, France). The website positions the company as a trusted local expert for construction, renovation, and outdoor landscaping projects — serving both residential and commercial clients across the Briochine area and up to 100 km radius.


## Outcome

Delivered a clear, conversion-optimized website that helps a local tradesperson stand out against competitors, attract qualified leads, and communicate professionalism and reliability — ultimately turning online visitors into confirmed construction projects.
