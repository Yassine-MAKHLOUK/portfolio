## Overview

Developed a high-end web site for AJ Prestiges Cars, a Paris-based luxury and sports car rental agency serving the Île-de-France region. The website reflects the brand’s premium positioning while providing a smooth, user-friendly browsing experience.

## Key Features & Responsibilities:
- **Dynamic vehicle filtering** – Users can search by year range and price (0–500,000€) to find their ideal car.
- **Service highlights** – Clearly presented unique selling points (loyalty program, 7/7 availability, French license plates, competitive pricing, ideal location, diverse fleet).
- **Wedding & event car rental section** – Tailored content for special occasion hires.
- **Showroom location & contact integration** – Easy access for clients across Île-de-France.

## Outcome:
A clean, conversion-focused website that establishes AJ Prestiges Cars as a trusted luxury service provider, helping them attract both local and visiting customers looking for premium vehicle rentals.
