## Overview

Developed the website for Chez Georges, a premium bistronomic restaurant located in the heart of Versailles (12 Rue du Vieux Versailles). The establishment offers a warm yet refined "rustic chic" atmosphere, combining authentic French gastronomy with innovative, seasonal cuisine.

## Key Features

- **Reservation & private dining focus** – Clear pathways for standard table bookings and venue privatization for special events (birthdays, corporate dinners, weddings).
- **Event personalization workflow** – Step-by-step explanation of the private event process: listening to client needs, custom menu creation with the chef, space preparation, and attentive service.
- **Contact & location** – Integrated contact form and Google Maps-ready address for easy reservations and walk-in discovery.

## Outcome

Delivered an elegant, conversion-driven website that increases reservation requests, promotes private event services, and positions Chez Georges as a go-to gastronomic destination in Versailles — balancing authenticity with modernity.
