## Overview

Developed a high-end website for Anna – Maison G.R.F., a modern French gastronomic restaurant located near Place de l'Étoile in Paris (17th arrondissement). Led by Chef Sylvain Dereau, the restaurant’s name stands for "Grillé Rôti Fondant" (Grilled Roasted Melt-in-the-Mouth), reflecting its culinary philosophy.

## Key Features

- **Menu presentation** – Elegant display of signature dishes.
- **Opening hours & location** – Clear weekly schedule (Mon–Sat, lunch & dinner) and interactive map showcasing the location of the restaurant.
- **Mobile-responsive design** – Optimized for guests searching on-the-go near Arc de Triomphe/Ternes area.

## Outcome

Delivered a refined, conversion-driven website that showcases the restaurant's identity, drives reservations, and positions Maison G.R.F. as a destination for both traditional and modern French cuisine lovers.
