## Overview

Developed a digital presence for Poissonnerie Ledreux, the oldest fishmonger in Paris (established over 120 years ago), located in the 14th arrondissement. The unique concept allows guests to select their fresh fish or seafood directly from the display, which the chef then prepares with custom seasoning and side dishes of their choice.

## Key Features

- **Hybrid concept presentation** – Clearly communicated the "choose then cook" model: pick your product from the stall, chef prepares it to your liking.
- **Heritage & trust** – Highlighted 120+ years of history and direct sourcing from the best fishing ports to establish authority and quality perception.
- **Restaurant & privatization** – Clear calls-to-action for table reservations (lunch/dinner) and private event bookings via phone or contact form.
- **Warm & convivial branding** – Conveyed a welcoming, friendly atmosphere paired with high-quality, passionate service.

## Outcome

Delivered a clean, appetite-driven website that showcases a one-of-a-kind Parisian institution, drives reservation requests, and bridges the gap between traditional fishmonger retail and modern restaurant dining — turning visitors into loyal customers.
